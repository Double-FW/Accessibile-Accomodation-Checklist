# Related public Double-FW guidance

These repositories were confirmed public through GitHub on 20 November 2026. The date records visibility at inspection, not permanent availability, source currency, adoption or completed assessment. Choose the source for the actual question and record the exact revision and heading used. This navigation does not bundle or relicense linked content.

| Question | Public resource | Useful contribution and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What do shared concepts and evidence labels mean? | [Terminology and Principles](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles) | Scoped terminology resolutions, glossary status and external-meaning boundaries. Most entry wording remains proposed. |
| How can information reduce avoidable cognitive demand? | [Cognitive UX Design](https://github.com/Double-FW/Cognitive-UX-Design) | Orientation, clarity, processing demands, choice and continuity for information and support. Digital interaction guidance does not establish building dimensions or a fire strategy. |
| How should maps and complex visual information be explained? | [Accessible Charts, Graphs and Maps](https://github.com/Double-FW/Accessible_Charts_Graphs_and_Maps) | Relevant meaning and alternative information routes. A visual map or short alternative text alone may not make a physical journey usable. |
| How should pre-arrival and room guides be produced? | [Accessible Digital and Printed Documents](https://github.com/Double-FW/Accessible_Digital_and_Printed_Documents) | Structure, formats, reading order and appropriate editions; verify actual delivered documents and audience use. |
| How should participant and occupant information be handled? | [Data Ethics and Disability](https://github.com/Double-FW/Data_Ethics_and_Disability) | Proportionate involvement, privacy and the distinction between needs and diagnosis. A link supplies no consent, legal basis or approved storage arrangement. |
| How can intended experience and brand promise be investigated? | [POUR for Branding Systems and CX](https://github.com/Double-FW/POUR-for-Branding-Systems-and-CX) | Satisfactory outcomes, agency, dignity and emotional research lenses. Ratings do not establish safe accommodation or remove access barriers. |
| How should commitments and responsibilities be written? | [Inclusion and Accessibility Policy](https://github.com/Double-FW/Inclusion-and-Accessibility-Policy) | Distinguish adopted commitments, responsibilities, proposals and evidence. Its secondary guidance is not a building-control decision or legal determination. |

## Use alongside the owning technical sources

These resources complement the [accommodation checklist](Accomodation_Checklist.md); they do not replace current legislation, statutory guidance, competent professional assessment, lawful access to relevant standards, or the site's actual operating/fire arrangements. Do not infer that a digital accessibility framework regulates a building merely because both concern access.

If a link or exact source is unavailable, record the limitation and keep the affected conclusion unknown. A 403, sign-in wall or retrieval error does not by itself establish that a repository is private or a source is absent. Inspect a genuinely relevant public replacement before relying on it. Do not reconstruct private content or add private dependencies to public training resources.
